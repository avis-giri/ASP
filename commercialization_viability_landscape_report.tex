\section{Purpose and Scope}

This report maps the current tool landscape serving two distinct but intersecting
markets:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Venture Capital firms} -- startup discovery, screening, and
        technical diligence for deep-tech and university-origin investments.
  \item \textbf{University Offices of Technology Management (OTM/TTO)} --
        invention disclosure intake, IP operations, licensing strategy, and
        commercialization triage decisions.
\end{itemize}

The analysis identifies where current platforms reach their functional limits,
where structural process gaps remain unaddressed, and where a purpose-built
commercialization viability platform can claim a defensible and differentiated
product position.

% ============================================================
\section{Executive Findings}

\begin{insightbox}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item[\textbf{1.}] \textbf{The market is fragmented by design.}
        VC and OTM teams each assemble heterogeneous tool stacks spanning CRM,
        market intelligence, patent analytics, publication retrieval, and
        compliance systems, with no native integrating layer connecting them.

  \item[\textbf{2.}] \textbf{No production platform closes the full loop.}
        No reviewed tool combines patent claim analysis, scientific evidence
        quality grading, market sizing, competitor mapping, and a composite
        commercialization viability score within a single decision workflow.

  \item[\textbf{3.}] \textbf{OTM triage remains manually bottlenecked.}
        Critical decisions -- patent filing, licensing path selection, case
        assignment, conflict-of-interest review -- are made without systematic
        scoring or auditable decision infrastructure.

  \item[\textbf{4.}] \textbf{The intervention point is structurally clear.}
        A governance-grade decision engine that integrates into existing OTM
        systems and produces investor-ready diligence outputs represents the
        highest-leverage position available in the current stack.
\end{enumerate}
\end{insightbox}

\subsection{VC Tooling: Conclusion}

\begin{opportunitybox}
  VC tooling is functionally mature in isolated components. Teams continue to
  manually combine outputs across market data products, IP tools, and external
  technical diligence providers. No production system synthesizes these inputs
  into a traceable, scored commercialization verdict for university-origin
  deep tech.
\end{opportunitybox}

\subsection{Regulatory and Process Baseline}

US universities managing federally funded inventions operate under Bayh-Dole Act
obligations, including mandatory disclosure, election-of-title timelines, and
iEdison reporting through NIST. These compliance requirements enforce structured
intake processes but provide no commercialization scoring, market validation, or
investor-grade viability output at any stage of the disclosure-to-license
workflow.

\section{Where the Current System Breaks Down}

Documented OTM workflow evidence confirms that manual judgment remains the
deciding factor at every high-stakes point in the commercialization process:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Case assignment} to licensing managers, based primarily on
        domain familiarity and available capacity.
  \item \textbf{Patent filing and prosecution decisions} made without
        standardized readiness thresholds or comparative market evidence.
  \item \textbf{Licensing strategy selection} -- exclusive vs.\ non-exclusive,
        startup spinout vs.\ established partner -- driven by individual manager
        judgment rather than evidence-backed scoring.
  \item \textbf{Conflict-of-interest and governance review} prior to deal
        execution, with no standardized record of the decision rationale.
  \item \textbf{Disclosure prioritization} under resource constraints, with no
        systematic scoring to guide sequencing across a live portfolio.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{insightbox}
  \textbf{This is the structural gap.} Systematic scoring and structured evidence
  packaging can improve decision consistency and processing speed without
  displacing accountable human oversight at final decision gates.
\end{insightbox}

\section{Gap Taxonomy and Intervention Design}

\subsection{Six Structural Gaps in the Current Stack}

\vspace{4pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.65}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{L{3.1cm} X}
  \rowcolor{headerbg}
  \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Gap}} &
  \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Description}} \\

  \rowcolor{rowlight}
  \textbf{Workflow\newline fragmentation} &
  VC and OTM systems operate in parallel silos with no shared decision layer or
  structured handoff between university triage and downstream investor diligence. \\

  \textbf{Readiness\newline framework} &
  TRL status views exist in some systems, but composite multi-dimensional
  commercialization readiness scores are not standardized or auditable across
  institutions or asset classes. \\

  \rowcolor{rowlight}
  \textbf{Evidence\newline normalization} &
  Disclosures, patent claims, scientific publications, and market data are
  ingested through separate systems and never converged into a single traceable
  evidence model. \\

  \textbf{Benchmarking} &
  Sparse external baselines exist for university-stage assets by domain and
  development stage, preventing calibrated scoring and comparative portfolio
  assessment. \\

  \rowcolor{rowlight}
  \textbf{Governance} &
  No production system maintains versioned decision logs, override documentation,
  or auditable scoring histories for disclosure-to-commercialization decisions. \\

  \textbf{Actionability} &
  Existing platforms do not prescribe prioritized de-risking actions tied to
  expected commercialization score improvement, leaving licensing managers to
  determine next steps without structured guidance. \\[4pt]
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Product Positioning}

\begin{insightbox}
  \textbf{Core thesis:} Build a commercialization viability decision engine that
  sits between OTM systems of record and downstream investor or partner diligence
  workflows. The platform ingests multi-source evidence -- patents, publications,
  market data, regulatory signals -- and produces explainable, structured
  commercialization verdicts with a complete audit trail.
\end{insightbox}

\textbf{Primary outputs delivered by the platform:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Explainable composite viability score with confidence level and
        per-dimension breakdown.
  \item Citation-backed evidence map spanning IP defensibility, scientific
        evidence quality, market sizing, and competitive landscape.
  \item Prioritized de-risking action plan with expected score delta per action.
  \item Committee-grade decision packet with version history and override log.
\end{itemize}

\section{Phased Build Plan}

\vspace{6pt}

\begin{phasebox}{Phase 1: MVP \normalfont\textcolor{midgray}{\small -- Months 0 to 4}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Read-only data connectors to one OTM source system, with CSV ingestion
        fallback for institutions not yet integrated.
  \item Scoring framework covering: technical maturity, market demand validation,
        IP defensibility, regulatory pathway clarity, scale feasibility, and
        team capability.
  \item Evidence extraction with source traceability and confidence-level flags
        on all scored dimensions.
  \item Output: one-page commercialization brief with supporting structured
        evidence appendix.
\end{itemize}
\end{phasebox}

\vspace{10pt}

\begin{phasebox}{Phase 2: Platform Integration \normalfont\textcolor{midgray}{\small -- Months 5 to 10}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Bidirectional workflow integration with the primary OTM stack in use at
        the pilot institution.
  \item Benchmarking module by technology domain and development stage.
  \item Portfolio triage interface with prioritization queues and decision
        governance controls for committee review.
\end{itemize}
\end{phasebox}

\vspace{10pt}

\begin{phasebox}{Phase 3: Network Intelligence \normalfont\textcolor{midgray}{\small -- Months 11 to 18}}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Cross-institution benchmark intelligence with institutional privacy
        controls and configurable data sharing boundaries.
  \item Predictive conversion modeling for license and startup progression
        outcomes based on scored evidence vectors.
  \item Counterparty-ready diligence output packages formatted for VC and
        strategic partner workflows.
\end{itemize}
\end{phasebox}

\textbf{Target site:} One research-intensive university OTM, co-piloting with one
affiliated translational funding committee.

\begin{itemize}

  \item Share of cases with an explicit, documented de-risking action plan.
  \item Early-stage conversion lift to formal license negotiation or startup
        diligence gate.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}

The evidence in this report supports the core product thesis. The market for
university-origin deep-tech commercialization tooling is large and not well
served. Existing platforms are capable in narrow verticals -- IP analytics,
market data, OTM workflow management -- but none deliver integrated
commercialization viability assessment that combines patent intelligence,
scientific evidence quality, market sizing, and governance-grade decision
infrastructure in a single product.

\vspace{6pt}
\begin{opportunitybox}
  A platform that combines explainable viability scoring, traceable evidence
  packaging, governance infrastructure, and bidirectional OTM workflow integration
  addresses a real and persistent operational gap for both university technology
  transfer offices and the deep-tech investors who depend on their output.
\end{opportunitybox}
